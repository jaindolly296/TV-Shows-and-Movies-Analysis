# 📺 Amazon Prime TV Shows & Movies Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Amazon Prime TV Shows & Movies dataset using Python. The objective is to clean the data, explore content trends, analyze genres, ratings, production countries, and generate meaningful business insights through visualizations.

The project demonstrates practical Data Analytics skills including data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, and data visualization.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Amazon Prime's content library and answer important business questions such as:

- What type of content is most common?
- Which genres are the most popular?
- How has content production changed over time?
- Which countries produce the most content?
- How are IMDb and TMDB ratings related?
- What is the distribution of movie runtimes?

The insights can help support content acquisition and strategic decision-making.

# 💡 Key Insights

- Movies dominate the Amazon Prime catalog.
- Content production increased significantly after 2015.
- Drama and Comedy are the most common genres.
- Most content is produced in the United States.
- Movie runtimes are concentrated between 80–120 minutes.
- IMDb and TMDB ratings show a positive relationship.
- Most content falls into adult-oriented age certifications.
